New Features:
• Two new ways to send: Briskwire™ Frame Mono and the fast animated Briskwire™ Glyph.
• Glyph sends megabytes, not kilobytes - a photo goes in one transfer.
• A 5 fps speed for older cameras, and a live capacity counter when you generate a code.
• Licences and their full texts in About, readable offline.

Improvements:
• Receiving moves the whole way instead of jumping to 100%.
• Accented and non-Latin text no longer scans back as "?".
