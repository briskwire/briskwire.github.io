New Features:
• Faster transfers — new BriskFrame and QR RGB modes (Beta) up to 3× quicker than plain QR.
• Frame loss recovery — built-in fountain coding fills small gaps automatically.
• Retry code — when frames are missing, show a small QR back to the sender and it re-broadcasts just those parts.
• Smarter scanner — adapts resolution, focus, and zoom on the fly.
• Per-frame progress dots — see exactly which frames have arrived.

Improvements:
• Looking away briefly no longer resets the scan — received frames are kept.
• More reliable on older phones — fixes memory crashes on dense scans.
• Clearer accessibility (hollow rings show missing frames, not just colour).

Bug Fixes:
• Settings dialog crash on certain themes.
• Retry button now shows a message when there's nothing to re-encode.
• Retry QR renders reliably for any number of missing frames.
