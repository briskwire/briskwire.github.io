New Features:
• First release — offline file transfer between two Android phones.
• Animated QR codes — send any file as a QR sequence the other device scans with its camera.
• NFC tap — hold two devices back-to-back to stream the file over short-range radio.
• Protocol picker — choose QR or NFC on every send.
• Received files — re-share, export, open in other apps, or delete.
