How to transfer files between phones without internet

One screen, one camera, zero networks. One phone shows animated QR codes and the other reads them with its camera. No Bluetooth pairing, no network, nothing to intercept.

Step 1 · Share

Pick a file

Share any file to Briskwire™ from your gallery, file manager or another app. Then choose Plain, or Encrypted for one paired receiver.

Step 2 · Send

Screen ➜ Camera

The sending phone plays the file as animated frames at full brightness. Choose QR, Frame Mono or the dense Glyph codec. The receiver can read the frames in any order.

Step 3 · Receive

Reassemble & verify

The other phone's camera reads the frames, and a progress grid shows what has arrived. If frames are missing, the receiver shows a retry code to the sender. The finished file is verified before you open it.

Made for the places phones go but the network doesn't

For sharing files where there is no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or mobile network. QR suits documents and keys, Glyph suits photos. Not for 4K video, but much more than text.

Hikers, climbers & outdoor crews

On a ridge or in a canyon, far from any signal. Send a GPX track to your partner, waypoints to the group, or a route plan or rescue PDF to the support van. No signal needed.

Field engineers, surveyors & inspectors

Industrial sites, refineries and substations, where mobile data is banned or has no coverage. Pass measurement files, calibration settings, handover notes and checklists from phone to phone without using any radio.

Privacy-conscious professionals

Journalists, lawyers, security researchers and founders. Move a contract, a password database or a private key. Briskwire™ has no INTERNET permission, so your data can only reach the receiving phone.

Travellers in airplane mode

On a plane, on a train across a border, or abroad without a local SIM. Give a boarding pass, an itinerary or a contact card to the person next to you in seconds. No roaming, no shared Wi-Fi, no messenger app.

Three ways to encode. Pick the one that fits.

All three send the same files. QR works with any camera, Frame Mono carries more per frame, and Glyph carries the most, using four colours.

Briskwire™ QR · universal

QR: works anywhere

The universal default. Any camera, any distance, any light. Best for small files such as documents, keys and settings.

Briskwire™ Frame Mono · dense

Frame Mono: denser

A denser black-and-white grid than QR. More data per frame and faster transfers with good cameras at close range.

Briskwire™ Glyph · densest

Glyph: densest

Four-colour symbols carry far more data per frame, so a photo goes in one transfer. A generated Glyph code with less data uses bigger cells and scans more easily.

Plus: colour QR for single codes

Briskwire™ QR RGB puts three QR codes into the red, green and blue channels of one image, for about 3× the data. Create it in Generate and scan it in Detect. Both codes below hold the same text.

Plain QR · 1 channel

Black & white

One bit per square. This is the format QR was designed for in 1994. It works on paper, but uses only one of the three colour channels that screens and cameras have.

Briskwire™ RGB · 3 channels

Colour (R + G + B)

Three QR codes in the red, green and blue channels of one image. About 3× the data in the same space. Best at close range in good light. Scan it in Detect.

Both codes hold the same text. Tap to see it and check.

How Briskwire™ compares to browser-based QR transfer tools

Several open-source web pages can also send files as animated QR codes. Briskwire™ makes a different promise: proof, not trust.

Provably offline, not just "offline-capable"

  • Briskwire™ has no INTERNET permission, so Android itself blocks all network access. Check it in Settings or with any APK inspector.
  • A browser tab always has network access. A web tool asks you to trust that it does not use it. Briskwire™ shows you that it cannot.

Missed frames come back

  • Browser tools send one way and hope enough frames arrive. In Briskwire™, the receiver shows a retry code and the sender resends only the missing frames, so the transfer can always finish.
  • The app tunes focus, resolution and zoom during the scan. Web pages cannot control these camera settings.

Encryption one-way tools cannot offer

  • Users of browser QR tools ask most for encryption, and those tools lack it. Briskwire™ encrypts a transfer end-to-end for one paired receiver, with a security code on both screens.
  • A one-way web page has no channel for exchanging keys. Briskwire™ uses both cameras to pair the phones, fully offline, with no server in between.

Built into Android, not a browser tab

  • Any app can share a file to Briskwire™. Received files go to a private inbox with Share, Export, Open in… and Delete.
  • Rotate the phone, switch apps or lose sight of the screen, and the scanner continues where it stopped. A browser tab cannot promise that.

About Briskwire™

Briskwire™ is for places where normal file sharing fails: a mountain ridge with no signal, a secure site where Wi-Fi is banned, a flight. It cannot reach the internet, because it never asks for that permission. No Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, cloud, account or telemetry.

Why animated codes?

You can see the frames, and they travel only as far as the light from the screen. Nothing announces your phone to others, and there is no network connection to intercept. You see exactly what the other phone shows: that is the whole trust model.

Encrypted, even over light

You can encrypt a transfer for one recipient. The sender scans the receiver's one-time key code, and the frames then carry only encrypted data, so someone filming the screen records noise. If someone were in the middle, the six-digit security codes on the two phones would differ. Plain mode is one tap away when you send to several receivers.

Privacy on first launch

On first launch, Briskwire™ shows its privacy policy and asks you to accept it. The policy is built into the app, so nothing is downloaded. If you decline, the app closes. Read the full privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers about offline file transfer, encryption, file sizes, supported barcodes and privacy.

Does Briskwire™ need Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or internet to transfer files?

No. One phone shows animated QR codes and the other phone's camera reads them. The app has no INTERNET permission, so it cannot go online. It works with every radio off, including in airplane mode.

Do both phones need Briskwire™ installed?

Yes, to send a file both phones need Briskwire™. To scan ordinary barcodes and single QR codes, one phone with the built-in Detect scanner is enough.

How large a file can I send?

It depends on the codec. QR is for small files such as documents and keys. Glyph sends photos and files up to tens of megabytes in one transfer. Video is not practical. Interrupted transfers can be resumed.

Are transfers encrypted?

Optionally, yes. In Encrypted mode, the sender scans the receiver's one-time key code and the frames carry only encrypted data. Both phones then show a six-digit security code. If the codes differ, discard the file. Keys are new for every transfer and never leave the two phones.

Which barcode formats can Briskwire™ scan?

QR, Aztec, Codabar, Code 39, Code 93, Code 128, Data Matrix, EAN-8, EAN-13, ITF, PDF417, UPC-A and UPC-E, plus Briskwire™ QR RGB. Links, phone numbers, emails, SMS, locations, contacts, calendar events and Wi-Fi networks open in the matching Android app with one tap.

Does Briskwire™ collect any data?

No. There are no analytics, crash reports, ads, trackers or accounts, and nothing leaves your phone. Received files and scan sessions stay in the app's private storage. See the privacy policy for details.

Which Android version do I need, and is there an iPhone version?

Briskwire™ runs on Android 11 and newer and is sold on Google Play as a one-time purchase. There is no iOS version.

What languages does the app support?

English, Polish, Ukrainian, Spanish and German. Every screen is translated, and you can change the language and theme in Settings.

How do I transfer files between two phones without internet?

Install Briskwire™ on both phones and share a file to it on the sending phone. Its screen plays animated frames. Point the other phone's camera at the screen, and Briskwire™ rebuilds and verifies the file. No Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data, cables or accounts are needed.

Is the QR code scanner free of ads, and does it work offline?

Yes. The built-in Detect scanner reads QR codes and common barcodes fully offline, with no ads, trackers or accounts. Links, phone numbers, Wi-Fi details, contacts and events open in Android's own apps.
